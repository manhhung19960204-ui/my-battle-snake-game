## INTRODUCE AND GAMEPLAY 
- There is two mode to play this game , SINGLE PLAY AND COMPUTER PLAY 
- SINGLE PLAY : You will control snake to eat apple , your snake will bigger every time you eat , and if you touch your tail , you will lose
- COMPUTER PLAY : You will control snake to eat apple and fight face to face COM SNAKE , it will eat like user and bigger too , at the end COM SNAKE have more score than user , it will WIN . User can use their tail to kill COM SNAKE  
- Snake alway go straigh and user use button up down to move and eat
